## 1. Introduction

Bayesian Inference is a statistical method that updates the probability estimate for a hypothesis as more evidence or information becomes available. It is widely used in various fields, including machine learning, to make predictions and decisions under uncertainty.

## 2. Prior Fitted Network (PFN)

The Prior Fitted Network (PFN) is a machine learning technique that combines the principles of Bayesian inference with the flexibility of neural networks. It allows for the incorporation of prior knowledge into the learning process, leading to more robust and interpretable models.
